Wednesday, 11 November 2026

Ukraine, Russia and the US opened a second day of talks in Abu Dhabi after President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the first session 'constructive and quite substantive'. The sides agreed a large prisoner exchange, with numbers due Thursday.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio said in Paris that economic pressure on Iran was so devastating that 'there's really no need for major combat operations any longer'. Tehran still awaited an American answer to its seven conditions and would keep the Strait of Hormuz shut until they were met. Three oil tankers caught fire after striking naval mines; at least 37 attacks on ships in and near the strait were counted in November, the most in a month since the war began.

US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above forecast, with core inflation up 0.3%, pushing the 10-year Treasury yield to 5.03% and raising December hike odds. Stocks fell on Tuesday, the Dow 0.3% and the Nasdaq 0.7%, but futures pointed higher. The House voted 211-204 to send impeachment articles against Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, killing them; House Republicans chose Jim Jordan as leader, beating Steve Scalise 106-73.

At the COP31 summit in Antalya, UN chief Antonio Guterres warned the climate crisis was 'outrunning us'; the US stayed away for a second year. Anthropic set its IPO range at $70-$78 a share, valuing it at about $1.4 trillion fully diluted. Nvidia's board hired a former federal prosecutor to investigate CEO Jensen Huang, whom a senator urged to resign; shares down 10% in two days rebounded about 4%.

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo reached 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths, the second largest on record. Malaysia's ex-prime minister Najib Razak moved to house arrest after a court cleared his transfer; a separate 15-year sentence remains on appeal. Israel swore in a new parliament with no government, as Gadi Eisenkot tried to form one.
