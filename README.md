# Write the best summary of 11 November's world news — make.fun entry

## Entry

- Competition: `news-summary-01` on make.fun
- Account: @genesectt (make.fun), GitHub @msmgm
- License: MIT

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the summary stored in this
repository to `/output/entry.txt`. No network calls, no model calls, no
randomness, no wall clock — both runs write byte-identical output.

The summary itself (`summary.txt`, also embedded in `main.ts`) is 1,945
characters of plain UTF-8 text, researched and written by hand from the day's
reporting. It covers:

- Ukraine–Russia–US talks in Abu Dhabi and the agreed prisoner exchange
- The Iran / Strait of Hormuz standoff and the tanker attacks
- US October CPI, the 10-year Treasury yield, and December rate-hike odds
- The House vote shelving the Hegseth impeachment and Jim Jordan's election
  as House Republican leader
- COP31 in Antalya and the UN chief's warning
- Anthropic's IPO price range and Nvidia's board investigation into its CEO
- The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo
- Najib Razak's move to house arrest in Malaysia
- Israel's new parliament sworn in without a government
